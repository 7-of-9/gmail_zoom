Pane Zoom for Gmail

Privacy Policy

Effective 12 November 2026

Pane Zoom for Gmail is a Chrome extension that zooms the email you're reading in Gmail, remembers the level per sender, and syncs those levels across your machines without a login. This page explains exactly what it handles.

What the extension reads, in your browser

  • The sender's address from the header of the message you have open, to pick that sender's zoom level.
  • Your Gmail address from the Gmail tab title, so each Gmail account keeps its own settings and can sync them.

It runs only on https://mail.google.com/. It doesn't read, store or send the content of your messages, your contacts, or anything else in your mailbox.

What it stores on your device

  • Your zoom levels, in Gmail's site storage in your browser, keyed by the hashes described below.
  • Whether sync is switched on, in the extension's own storage.

What it sends, and where

When sync is on, the extension sends this to its sync server at https://panezoom-sync.azurewebsites.net, hosted on Microsoft Azure (Southeast Asia):

  • An account key: a one-way SHA-256 hash of your Gmail address.
  • For each sender you've set a level for, a sender key: a one-way SHA-256 hash of your Gmail address together with the sender's address.
  • The zoom levels (numbers such as 1.5), and the time each one was set or deleted.

No email addresses, names, message content or browsing data are sent. The server stores one small record per account key. There are no analytics, no tracking and no ads. As with any web request, Microsoft's infrastructure sees your IP address to deliver it; the sync service doesn't record or use it.

The limitation of having no login

Sync works without an account because your Gmail address is the identity. The trade-off: someone who already knows your Gmail address could compute your account key, and could then check whether you've set a zoom level for a sender they guess, or change your levels. They can't list who emails you, and they can't read any mail. If that matters to you, switch sync off (below).

Your controls

Right-click the extension's toolbar icon:

  • Sync zoom levels across machines (a checkbox): untick it to stop syncing. Your levels then stay in this browser only.
  • Delete synced zoom levels (all machines): erases the per-sender levels and the default level for every Gmail account this browser has used. They're erased on the server immediately, and on each of your other machines at its next sync: the deletion carries a timestamp, and anything set before it is dropped everywhere. Sync stays on for changes you make afterwards; use the checkbox above to turn it off.

How long data is kept

Synced levels are kept until you delete them. After a deletion, only an empty record named by the account hash remains. Records that haven't been touched for a year are removed automatically.

Sharing

The data isn't sold, shared with or transferred to anyone, and isn't used for anything other than syncing your zoom levels. Pane Zoom for Gmail's use of information complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Open source

All code, including the sync server, is public at github.com/7-of-9/gmail_zoom, so you can check every claim on this page.
