Pane Zoom for Gmail

Privacy Policy

Effective 13 November 2026

Pane Zoom for Gmail is a Chrome extension that zooms the email you're reading in Gmail, shows every message at one text size, and syncs your zoom level across your machines without a login. This page explains exactly what it handles.

What the extension reads, in your browser

  • Your Gmail address from the Gmail tab title, so each Gmail account keeps its own level and can sync it.

It runs only on https://mail.google.com/ and changes how the open message, its subject and your drafts are displayed (text size and zoom). It doesn't read, store or send the content of your messages, who sends them, your contacts, or anything else in your mailbox.

What it stores on your device

  • Your zoom level, in Gmail's site storage in your browser, keyed by the account hash described below.
  • Whether sync is switched on, in the extension's own storage.

What it sends, and where

When sync is on, the extension sends this to its sync server at https://panezoom-sync.azurewebsites.net, hosted on Microsoft Azure (Southeast Asia):

  • An account key: a one-way SHA-256 hash of your Gmail address.
  • Your zoom level (a number such as 1.5), and the time it was set or deleted.

Versions before 1.4 kept a level per sender and synced one-way hashes of sender addresses with them. Version 1.4 deletes those automatically, on the server and on each of your machines.

No email addresses, names, message content or browsing data are sent. The server stores one small record per account key. There are no analytics, no tracking and no ads. As with any web request, Microsoft's infrastructure sees your IP address to deliver it; the sync service doesn't record or use it.

The limitation of having no login

Sync works without an account because your Gmail address is the identity. The trade-off: someone who already knows your Gmail address could compute your account key, and could then see or change your zoom level. Nothing else is stored, so there's nothing else to see. If that matters to you, switch sync off (below).

Your controls

Right-click the extension's toolbar icon:

  • Sync zoom levels across machines (a checkbox): untick it to stop syncing. Your level then stays in this browser only.
  • Delete synced zoom levels (all machines): erases the zoom level for every Gmail account this browser has used. They're erased on the server immediately, and on each of your other machines at its next sync: the deletion carries a timestamp, and anything set before it is dropped everywhere. Sync stays on for changes you make afterwards; use the checkbox above to turn it off.

How long data is kept

Your synced level is kept until you delete it. After a deletion, only an empty record named by the account hash remains. Records that haven't been touched for a year are removed automatically.

Sharing

The data isn't sold, shared with or transferred to anyone, and isn't used for anything other than syncing your zoom level. Pane Zoom for Gmail's use of information complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Open source

All code, including the sync server, is public at github.com/7-of-9/gmail_zoom, so you can check every claim on this page.
