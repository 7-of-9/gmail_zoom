Pane Zoom for Gmail

Zoom the email, not the whole of Gmail.

A small Chrome extension that puts a − % + control on Gmail's reading pane. It enlarges just the message you're reading, remembers the level for each sender, and brings it to every machine you use Gmail on. No login.

Why

Chrome's zoom is all or nothing

⌘+ blows up the whole Gmail window: inbox, sidebars, toolbars. You wanted bigger text in one email.

Some mail is just small

Newsletters set in 12px, dense notification threads, receipts with 10px fine print. Squinting isn't a workflow.

Every sender is different

The newsletter you read closely wants 150%; notifications are fine at 110%. Set each once and forget it.

What it does

Per-sender zoom, remembered

The level sticks to whoever sent the message. Senders you haven't set yet open at the last level you picked.

Keyboard shortcuts

⌘⇧+ / ⌘⇧− on Mac, Ctrl+Shift+= / Ctrl+Shift+− on Windows and Linux. Click the % to go back to 100%.

All your machines, no login

Your Gmail account is the identity. Install it on your laptop and your desktop and the levels follow you automatically.

Wide emails fit the pane

Fixed-width newsletters and Outlook tables reflow to the pane when zoomed, instead of running off the side.

One text size

Headings, small print and the subject line are evened out, so the message reads at one size.

Or load it unpacked from GitHub

  1. Download or clone github.com/7-of-9/gmail_zoom.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode (top right).
  3. Click Load unpacked and pick the folder.
  4. Reload your Gmail tab and open an email.

Works in Chrome and other Chromium browsers (Edge, Brave, Arc).

Privacy, plainly

The extension runs only on mail.google.com. It reads the sender's address from the header of the email you have open, to pick the zoom, and your Gmail address from the tab title, to know whose settings these are. It never reads or sends message content.

To sync, it sends a small Azure server only one-way SHA-256 hashes of your Gmail address and of sender addresses, plus zoom numbers and timestamps. No names, no addresses, no mail content, no analytics, no ads.

The trade-off of no login: someone who already knows your Gmail address could check whether you've zoomed a sender they guess, or change your levels. They can't list who emails you or read any mail.

Right-click the toolbar icon to switch off Sync zoom levels across machines, or to Delete synced zoom levels (all machines).

FAQ

Does it read my email?

No. It reads only the sender's address in the header of the open message, to pick the zoom, and it hashes that address before anything leaves your browser. Message text never leaves your browser.

Do I need an account or a login?

No. The Gmail account you're signed in to is the identity, so the same settings appear on every machine where you install the extension and open that Gmail account.

Does it work with several Gmail accounts?

Yes. Each account has its own settings, and each syncs on its own.

Google Workspace accounts?

Yes, including Workspace accounts with a custom-branded Gmail.

Can I turn sync off?

Yes. Right-click the toolbar icon and untick “Sync zoom levels across machines”. Your levels then stay in this browser only.

What does it cost?

Nothing. It's free and open source under the MIT license.
